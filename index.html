Oh, hello!
I'm Hannah.

A materials science graduate finding her way through engineering — while collecting a lot of stories along the way.

My path started with a love of design, led me into materials research at UC San Diego, and eventually brought me to thermal management and liquid cooling technology. I like figuring out how things work, whether it's a tiny sensor, a piece of hardware, or something completely unexpected.

02 / SCIENCE CLUB

From the lab.

Science, trial and error, a lot of questions — and the joy of finally understanding something new.

MATERIALS SCIENCE & ENGINEERING

Flexible electronics & sensing.

During my master's studies at UC San Diego, I worked on flexible strain-sensing devices using carbon-nanotube-based materials, looking at how structure and deformation influence electrical response.

My favorite part of research? Moving between making samples, testing them, and figuring out what the data is trying to say.

  • FOCUS Flexible sensors & nanocomposites
  • TOOLS Fabrication, mechanical & electrical tests
  • INTEREST Structure–property relationships
  • ALSO Functional materials & manufacturing

Make it. Test it. Learn. Repeat.

03 / HOW DOES THAT WORK?

Inside the machine.

From tiny materials to big cooling challenges.

These days I'm learning about thermal management for computing hardware: cold plates, liquid cooling, manufacturing processes, and how engineering drawings turn ideas into things you can actually build.

I like to approach each design from four sides: thermal & fluid performance, materials & manufacturing, mechanical detail, and testing & reliability.
