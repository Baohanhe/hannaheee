HANNAH!CHAPTER 01 · THE PERSON / ABOUT
01 / THE PERSON

Hi, I’m Hannah.

I started out interested in fashion and design, then moved into materials science because I wanted to understand why things behave the way they do. My later research focused on flexible electronics and how materials and interfaces affect electrical response.

After graduating from UC San Diego, I started exploring liquid cooling for AI servers. It means learning a lot of mechanical drawings, manufacturing processes, and thermal fundamentals from scratch. I keep a notebook and try to understand one thing at a time.

Outside of that, I like hiking, tennis, taking photos, and being near the ocean. I miss San Diego, and I still find myself looking for chances to go back.

RESEARCH PROFILE / MATERIALS SCIENCE

From material structure to device response.

Most of my research has been a version of the same question: how do choices in materials and processing affect performance?

Baohan (Hannah) He · M.S., Materials Science & Engineering, UC San Diego

RESEARCH EXPERIENCE: 2023–2026 · PUBLICATIONS: 2023–2024
EDITOR’S SUMMARY

My background covers CNT-based strain sensors, functional materials and coatings, and rubber formulations. I’ve worked on synthesis, mechanical and electrical characterization, experimental troubleshooting, and interpreting structure–property relationships.

01 / UC SAN DIEGO · ARMOR LAB

Flexible strain sensing

OCT 2025 — JUN 2026

I designed and fabricated CNT-based flexible sensing devices and tested their response under tensile and cyclic loading. I examined how nanocomposite structure and sensor–skin interfaces influence the electrical signal, using MATLAB to analyze the data. I also worked with the team on motion-capture and soft-robotics applications.

02 / CHINESE ACADEMY OF SCIENCES

Functional materials & coatings

MAR — JUL 2024

At the Fujian Institute of Research on the Structure of Matter, I supported applied development of 3D-printing materials and protective coatings. My work included experimental planning, material procurement, process benchmarking, and sharing results with industry collaborators.

03 / FUJIAN NORMAL UNIVERSITY

Rubber formulations for shock absorbers

MAR 2023 — MAR 2024

I led a student research project on rubber formulations for automotive shock absorbers. We focused on mechanical resilience, dynamic-load response, and wear resistance, and I presented the work through a paper, poster, and thesis defense.

HANNAH!CHAPTER 03 · ENGINEERING / WORK NOTES
03 / THE WORKSHOP

Learning to keep
servers cool.

My current focus is liquid cooling for AI servers — cold plates, manifolds, quick disconnects, and the manufacturing and testing that make a cooling system reliable.

Coming from materials science, the mechanical side was new to me. I am trying to read drawings more professionally and understand what each dimension, material choice, and test requirement means in practice.

Cold platesLiquid coolingCAD drawingsManufacturingTesting & reliability
THE FOUR-ANGLE DRAWING METHOD

One drawing. Four questions.

01 /

Heat & flow

Where does heat enter? How is coolant distributed?

02 /

Materials & processes

What is the material, and how can this feature be manufactured?

03 /

Dimensions & assembly

Which tolerances control fit, interfaces, and sealing?

04 /

Tests & reliability

How will we verify leak tightness, cleanliness, and consistency?

OUTDOORS / HIKING

A reason to keep walking.

I like long hikes. The good parts are the views, but sometimes the hard parts are what I remember most.

Read note ↗

On my 25th birthday I climbed Half Dome. I was already exhausted by the time I reached the subdome. I started on the cables, got scared, and went back down. After a long pause I tried again. The mountain did not get any easier, but it was a day I wanted to remember.

SAN DIEGO / OCEAN

I still miss San Diego.

I used to live close to the ocean. Now that I have moved away, I notice how much I miss being able to walk down to the water whenever I wanted.

Read note ↗

When I was at UCSD, going to the ocean was a very ordinary thing. I could go for a swim or just sit on the coast. Now I live farther away, and the ocean is something I have to plan for. Maybe that is why I keep taking pictures of it.

UNDERWATER / EXPLORING

A completely different view.

I like trying things I do not know much about yet. Getting into the water and looking around is a good reminder that there is more out there than my screen and my desk.

Read note ↗

I am not trying to turn every hobby into a skill or a project. Sometimes I just want to see what a place looks like from a different angle. Getting into the water and watching fish move past was enough for the day.
